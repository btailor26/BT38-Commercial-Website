About BT38 Technology

Your business. Connected through BT38.

BT38 Technology provides self-hosted, white-label ecommerce systems, inventory management, marketplace integrations and workflow automation, configured around how your business operates.

About our company

Connected operations. Practical technology.

Our focus is bringing stock, orders, fulfilment and business systems together. We work with ecommerce businesses, brands and commercial partners to define the connections and workflows they need, from an individual API integration to a branded ecommerce platform.

When stock is spread across channels, orders need fulfilment and staff repeat the same tasks, disconnected systems can cost time and sales opportunities. We start with those operational problems and agree how your software should address them.

Branded under your own business name.

Your system carries your business name, logo and brand colours, with a customised interface for your team. White-label delivery means the workspace is presented as your own branded system.

Our self-hosted, white-label ecommerce systems bring inventory, marketplace orders and fulfilment into a workspace configured for your business. Unlimited SKUs, users and warehouse locations give your operation room to grow without BT38 charging per user or product.

Own your customer relationships and control access for staff and virtual assistants. Connect agreed marketplaces and web stores through API integrations, with bespoke workflow automation where your existing platforms leave gaps.
