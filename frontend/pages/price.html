Other applicable third-party subscriptions, AI API usage, marketplace charges and carrier fees remain subject to the agreed terms.

Unlimited MCF: No BT38-imposed dispatch limit. Amazon fulfilment charges still apply.

Delivery: Timeframes are based on agreed requirements, compatible API access and the customer providing the necessary information and credentials.
