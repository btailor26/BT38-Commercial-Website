Frequently asked questions

Your questions. Answered.

Understand your system, your ownership and the support available as your business grows.

Choosing the right system

What is the difference between an off-the-shelf and a bespoke system?

An off-the-shelf system comes with predefined features and workflows. Your business may need to adapt to the software. A bespoke system is designed around your business processes, responsibilities and requirements. BT38 offers an established core system that can be extended or customised to suit your operation.

Do I need to replace my existing systems?

Not necessarily. We review your existing systems first and identify what can be retained, connected or improved. We only recommend replacement where it makes sense for your business.

Can you build a system for my specific industry?

Yes, subject to an agreed scope. We work with people who understand your industry and day-to-day operations so the system reflects how your business actually works.

Can I start with a smaller package and expand later?

Yes. Additional integrations, workflows and features can be scoped and quoted as your business grows.

Can I see a demonstration before deciding?

Contact us to discuss your requirements and arrange a suitable demonstration.

Ownership and hosting

What does self-hosting mean?

Your system runs in a hosting account under your control, with your own database. If you manage the system yourself, you are responsible for hosting and associated third-party charges. If you choose BT38 monthly management from the outset, hosting is included in your agreed monthly management fee.

Do I need technical knowledge to manage a self-hosted system?

You do not need to be a developer to use the system. Installation, configuration, training and documentation are included within the agreed delivery scope. Technical hosting and maintenance can be managed separately if you prefer.

Can I use my own company name and logo?

Yes. The white-label system is branded around your business, including your logo and agreed brand colours.

Will I receive the source code?

Full source code handover is included in the Complete Bespoke Build. The Essential and Connected packages do not include full source code handover.

Are there monthly licence fees?

There is no compulsory monthly BT38 platform subscription or ongoing BT38 licence fee for the advertised system packages. Monthly management is optional. If agreed from the outset, hosting is included in your monthly management fee. Other third-party charges remain subject to the agreed terms.

Integrations and daily operations

Can you connect my existing marketplaces, carriers and accounting systems?

Yes, where compatible integrations and permissions are available. We agree the platforms and scope before development begins.

Can I manage multiple warehouses and staff members?

Yes. The system packages include unlimited users and warehouse locations, with staff roles and permissions. Advanced warehouse placement and staff workflows are available in the higher packages.

Can I access the system when I am away from the office?

Yes, through a compatible internet-connected device, subject to your hosting, security and access arrangements.

Can you integrate AI into my business?

Yes. We can connect supported AI services and scope custom AI workflows. Existing subscriptions may not include API access, and third-party usage charges may apply.

Can you automate customer service?

Yes. The Connected and Complete Bespoke packages can include agreed automated customer service workflows where required, including voice or text enquiries and escalation to staff.

Support and monthly management

Can you manage my system for me on a monthly basis?

Yes. We can create a bespoke monthly management package based around the level of support your business needs. This may include technical maintenance, agreed monitoring, system administration, operational support and ongoing improvements. We agree the responsibilities, scope and monthly cost with you.

If I choose monthly management from the start, do I pay separately for hosting?

No. If you choose a BT38 monthly management package from the outset, hosting is included within your agreed monthly management fee. You will not receive a separate hosting charge from BT38.

Your monthly management package will be tailored to your business, based on the support, responsibilities and services agreed in your management terms.

Do I have to take out a monthly management package?

No. You can manage your own system or request support as needed. Monthly management is optional.

What support is included after handover?

The three system packages include three months of technical support for faults or issues with the agreed delivered functionality, starting from handover.

What happens after the included support period?

You can request pay-as-you-need support or agree a monthly management arrangement. Charges are agreed before additional work begins.

Can you make changes to my system after it has been delivered?

Yes. New features, integrations and workflow improvements can be scoped and quoted separately.

Pricing and getting started

How much does a system cost?

The Essential System is £4,999, the Connected System is £9,999 and the Complete Bespoke Build is £19,999. Additional requirements are agreed and priced separately.

What is included in the £99 Business Systems Audit?

A 60-minute consultation, review of your existing systems and workflows, written recommendations within three working days and one month of clarification support.

How long does development take?

The Essential package is planned within one month, Connected up to two months and Complete Bespoke up to four months, subject to agreed requirements, access and customer information.

Are there any additional costs?

If you manage your own system, hosting and database services may incur separate charges. If you choose monthly management from the outset, hosting is included in the agreed monthly management fee. Marketplace and carrier charges, third-party subscriptions and API usage remain subject to your agreed terms. We agree additional development and support costs before work begins.

Do you work with businesses outside the UK?

Yes. We welcome international enquiries and assess the required regional platforms, integrations and business requirements as part of the project scope.

How do I get started?

Contact us with your requirements or book a Business Systems Audit if you need help identifying the right approach.
